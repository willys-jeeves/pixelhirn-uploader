Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

PixelhirnGaming Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool operated for the YouTube channel PixelhirnGaming. It uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

  • OAuth credentials for the channel owner's own YouTube channel, granted with the scopes youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly.
  • The channel's own video metadata and statistics (titles, descriptions, privacy status, view and like counts).

The app does not access, collect or store data of any other YouTube users or viewers. It has no user accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.

How data is used and stored

Credentials and channel statistics are stored only on the operator's private server, with file permissions restricted to the operator, and are used solely to upload the channel's own videos, update their metadata and publication status, and review the channel's own performance. No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept only as long as needed for reviewing the channel. Stored credentials are deleted when the app is no longer used. Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
